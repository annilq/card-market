## Design Specificity Verdict

**Authored:** the token layer. **Category-default:** every page composition.

Product-specific and measured: price as the only warm object (`#ec003f`, 16px/600, 4.53:1 on white, 6.58:1 on night paper, stepping to 24px/600 on detail); the `面议` slot occupying the same box and weight as a price so an unpriced card does not look priced; URL-as-state with cascading facets; two deliberately different announcement shapes.

Category-default: home = hero carousel → notices → horizontal strip → IP pills → 4-up grid, all equal weight, **no h1 and no sentence saying what the business does**. 260px filter rail of 16 checkboxes, 1:1 detail split, centered 404 — an unmodified commerce skeleton. Nothing structural is about buyback; it exists as a 10px amber badge over a strip showing *sale* prices. A seller cannot tell charge from pay, and no page, section, or sentence explains scope, condition, or how to submit.

## Overall Impression

A well-built system wrapped around an under-designed product. The token layer is real and it holds under measurement; the composition could belong to any TCG shop, and the one thing this business actually is — a buyback counter — is the least designed thing on the site.

## What's Working

1. **The price thesis survives measurement.** `#ec003f` at 4.53:1 on white, `#ff637e` at 6.58:1 on night paper, identical slot in strip / grid / detail. Nothing else is warm, so "what does it cost" resolves pre-attentively. The one claim the design makes, the render delivers.
2. **Token discipline holds under contact.** 45 focusables on `/cards`, every price / name / faint / border resolving through `@theme` + `html.dark` with no per-component overrides in `CardCard`, `HomeNotices`, `Pagination`, `message`. Dark is a real second reading, not an inversion.
3. **320px is honest and verified.** 7 routes at 320×640: `scrollWidth === 320`. Header 109px, cards 136px, an injected 481px Japanese name ellipsizes in a 145px box. `min-w-0` + `minmax(0,1fr)` is why.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] The X → Japanese-phone journey is broken at both ends
**What:** `__root.tsx:59-79` emits one `<title>` per locale for every route — measured identical on `/`, `/cards`, `/cards/1`, `/announcements/1`. No `og:title`, `og:image`, `og:url`, or `twitter:card` (only `og:locale`). `hreflang` alternates are **relative** (`href="/cards/1"`), which crawlers discard. No canonical. `<link rel="icon" href="data:,">`.
On the page: `/ja/cards` chrome is Japanese while banner, card names, announcements, site name (卡牌回购站) and footer legal all come from CMS in Chinese — `/en/cards` carries 20 CJK leaves under `lang="en"`, none marked `lang="zh"`. And the locale control measures **0×0 at 390px** (verified: `w:0,h:0`, parent `display:none`) until the hamburger opens.
**Why it matters:** the stated acquisition channel is an X post to a phone. That share is a naked grey link, and what it opens reads as a machine-translated reseller.
**Fix:** per-route `head()` with card name + price; 1200×630 `og:image`; absolute `og:url` / canonical / `hreflang`; `twitter:card`; real favicon + apple-touch-icon. Promote locale to a visible 中/日/EN control on mobile. Decide the content-language story: per-locale CMS fields with a fallback notice, or ship unilingual JA.
**Command:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P1] 咨询此卡 is a dead drop — the only conversion in the product loses the card
**What:** detail CTA href is literally `/message` (verified). The form has no card reference, so the highest-intent tap lands on a blank box that asks the user to retype the card. All three labels have `htmlFor: null` and do not wrap their controls — the accessible name comes only from the placeholder ("选填", "邮箱 / 电话 / 微信（选填）"). Success has **0** `role=status` / `aria-live` / `role=alert` regions. Failure renders `提交失败：TypeError: Failed to fetch` — raw internals, Chinese on all three locales.
**Why it matters:** every browse path ends here. A screen-reader user hears no field name and gets no confirmation that anything was sent.
**Fix:** pass card context and render a removable chip (`皮卡丘 · 编号 197/197 · ¥120`); associate labels with `id`/`htmlFor`; `role="status"` on success, `role="alert"` on failure; human error copy with a next step, detail to console.
**Command:** `/impeccable clarify`

### [P1] `text-faint` is 2.56:1 — and DESIGN.md blesses it
**What:** `#94a3b8` on white at 12px = **2.56:1**, **83 instances** across 9 pages: series line, card number, rarity, dates, totals, footer hint, copyright, search placeholder (2.55:1). Dark equivalent `#64748b` on `#1e293b` = **3.07:1**. `高价买取` badge 2.84:1, `置顶` 3.61:1 at 10px, `暂无图` glyph 1.36:1. Source: `src/styles.css:18` / `:45`, blessed at `DESIGN.md:178`.
**Why it matters:** this is the metadata used to tell two Pikachus apart, and it is the least readable text on the page. The design system encodes the bug, so every future screen inherits it.
**Fix:** split the token — keep `#94a3b8` for icons and dividers, add `--color-faint-text` (`#64748b` light / `#94a3b8` dark) for text and repoint card meta, dates, counters, footer. Darken amber to `#b45309`, raise the pinned red, lift `暂无图` to `text-faint`. Then amend `DESIGN.md:178`.
**Command:** `/impeccable audit`

### [P1] "Condition" is packaging, and the price is written in cents
**What:** every locale asks the buyer's third question — `成色` / `カードの状態` / **`Condition`** — and answers `裸卡` / `有包装` (`Raw card` / `In packaging`). That is packaging, not condition; a collector learns nothing about centering, whitening, or grade. Prices render `¥880.00` (`priceTag: '¥{price}'` over a raw decimal, all three dictionaries).
**Why it matters:** "what condition" is one of the three questions the whole system claims to answer, and the product cannot answer it.
**Fix:** rename the current field to packaging; add a real grade (A/B/C or NM/EX/VG) plus notes, shown as a second `dl` row, faceted beside 稀有程度, with a one-line scale legend. Format JPY with `maximumFractionDigits: 0`.
**Command:** `/impeccable clarify` (second pass)

### [P2] `/cards?page=5` is a dead end, and 清空 does nothing
**What:** with 6 cards and page size 24, `?page=5` renders `共 6 张` directly above `没有符合条件的卡牌`, an empty grid, and **no pagination at all** (`Pagination.tsx:18` returns `null` when `totalPages <= 1`) — no visible way back. On `?q=zzzz`, clicking 清空 left the URL and the count unchanged (`clearAll` at `cards.index.tsx:160` preserves `q` while `hasFilter` counts it); verified `?q=zzzz | 共 0 张` before and after. The button is 24×16px.
**Why it matters:** a stale shared link becomes a page that contradicts itself, and the one escape hatch does nothing.
**Fix:** clamp or redirect out-of-range pages in the loader; when `total === 0` show `0 张` with a working clear that also drops `q` ("清除搜索「zzzz」"); give it ≥24px height.
**Command:** `/impeccable polish`

## Minor Observations

1. `¥880.00` two decimals everywhere — costs real width in a 136px card at 320.
2. No `h1` on `/` or `/cards` while `/shops`, `/announcements`, `/message`, `/info/*` all have one; `/nope` and all four `/info/*` pages have no heading at all.
3. Announcement dates render `—` for both rows (`published_at` null): a dated list where every entry is undated reads as abandoned.
4. Detail repeats rarity twice (pill beside the price + 稀有程度 row) while giving that row's slot to packaging.
5. 返回列表 links without search, so the filters you arrived with are gone.
6. 500 page prints `error.message` to visitors (`__root.tsx:145`) at 2.56:1 — leaks internals illegibly.
7. No `prefers-reduced-motion` block anywhere; 32 transitioning elements including `group-hover:scale-[1.02]`. The only `focus-visible` ring in the system is on the high-value strip.
8. `SHOP` hardcoded Latin at ≥1024 (`__root.tsx:235`) while the same destination is 店铺 / 店舗 elsewhere; `固定` for 置顶 is not how a Japanese shop writes it. Header `<nav>` has no `aria-label` (footer's does). No skip link.
9. Banner autoplay (5s, `index.tsx:48-52`) has no pause control — latent WCAG 2.2.2 the moment the client adds a second banner.
10. Touch targets under 24px: `全部 →` 44×20, `← 返回首页` 72×17, `浏览卡牌` 56×20; filter checkbox labels 354×**20**.
11. Perf is clean where it ships: production bundle 189 KB gzip total vs 6.47 MiB cold on the dev server (a dev artifact — do not grade the design on it). Console and network: **0 errors** on all 9 pages.

## Questions to Consider

1. If the three answers are have-it / what-it-costs / what-condition, why does the card page spend 475 of 844 vertical pixels on an image that is the same placeholder for six different cards, with the price at y=734 and the only CTA at y=968?
2. What would the homepage look like built for the seller — one number ("we pay up to ¥XX,XXX"), four accepted categories, one form? Today buyback is a 10px badge plus a strip of sale prices, and no one can tell charge from pay.
3. A site that is explicitly not e-commerce has exactly one conversion: a free-text box that does not know which card you were looking at and does not promise a reply. Is that a catalogue, or a PDF with routing?
4. If Japanese is the launch language, why is the language control 0×0 on the device and entry point launch depends on, and why does `/ja` still say 新到宝可梦 151 全图鉴?
